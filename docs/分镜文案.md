# 挪威七月二十二日：180 秒分镜与旁白草稿

15 × 12 秒；沿用参考图 16:9 横版。旁白须在看过实际生成画面后修订、实测配音时长；此稿不是已同步字幕。

## 01｜000–012 秒｜三秒警报
七十七条生命，一天之内消逝。二〇一一年七月二十二日，挪威接连遭遇两起袭击。城市里的爆炸声后，更大的悲剧正在逼近一座小岛。

0–3s: hard cut from blue emergency light reflecting in shattered office glass to a dropped umbrella on wet pavement. No strobe. 3–7s: civilian-level view of damaged Oslo government quarter, drifting dust, emergency workers moving. 7–12s: cut to an overcast Norwegian lake, a distant wooded island, water rippling. No nuclear cloud, no collapse, no bodies.

## 02｜012–024 秒｜凶手并非传说
实施袭击的，是安德斯·贝林·布雷维克。这不是所谓天才罪犯的传奇，而是一名极右翼极端分子，将仇恨变成现实暴力的恐怖袭击。

A sober investigative reconstruction. 0–5s: anonymous adult blond Norwegian man, early thirties, seen only from behind in an ordinary 2011 apartment corridor. 5–12s: empty austere room with closed laptop, curtains moving. No smile, no heroic lighting, no extremist insignia, no manifesto text.

## 03｜024–036 秒｜预谋留下的阴影
这场袭击并非临时起意。事前准备与极端思想交织，最终指向无辜的人。我们不复述他的宣言，也不展示作案方法，只追问悲剧如何发生。

0–6s: investigative evidence archive, a gloved adult archivist closes an unlabelled file, a wall clock moves. 6–12s: rain runs down an ordinary farmhouse window, empty interior beyond. No chemicals, explosives, weapon assembly, legible writings or tactical maps.

## 04｜036–048 秒｜十五点二十五分
下午三点二十五分，奥斯陆政府办公区发生爆炸。八人在这里遇难。街道被碎玻璃和烟尘笼罩，救援人员冲进现场，寻找仍然需要帮助的人。

Oslo government quarter reconstruction in July 2011. 0–4s: dust blows across broken office windows in a damaged but standing concrete building. 4–12s: two adult rescue workers move along a street with scattered glass and guide an unharmed adult away. No visible injuries, no mushroom cloud, no building collapsing.

## 05｜048–060 秒｜危险转向湖面
爆炸之后，危险没有结束。布雷维克离开奥斯陆，前往于特岛。那里正在举办工党青年组织的夏令营，岛上的人还不知道，袭击者正向他们靠近。

0–5s: anonymous unmarked ordinary 2011 passenger car travels on a Norwegian road; no police livery, no speeding spectacle. 5–12s: ferry landing across a grey lake toward a small forested island; rain ripples and trees sway. Observational, no tactical route or map.

## 06｜060–072 秒｜本该普通的一天
于特岛原本是一处相聚的地方。年轻人在这里参加夏令营，讨论政治，也憧憬未来。那天本该留下的是谈笑与回忆，而不是此后一生难忘的恐惧。

Overcast damp July Norwegian youth camp, not golden sunny weather. 0–6s: a few unidentifiable older teenagers and young adults talk quietly under a canopy, modest 2011 casual clothes. 6–12s: hands close a guitar case beside camp chairs; lake beyond. Subtle warm indoor light contrasted with cold exterior. No violence.

## 07｜072–084 秒｜被利用的信任
傍晚，袭击者穿着警察样式的制服，乘船抵达于特岛。他利用人们对警察的信任接近营地。最先遭到袭击的，包括营地负责人和一名警卫。

0–5s: ferry moors, a single anonymous adult in dark police-like clothing walks onto a jetty viewed from distant rear. 5–8s: two adult camp staff look toward the arrival. 8–12s: cut away to empty jetty as a rope swings. No visible gunshot, no injuries, no authentic identifiable victim likeness, no glamorized attacker.

## 08｜084–096 秒｜七十三分钟
下午五点二十一分左右，岛上的枪击开始。直到六点三十四分，警方才将袭击者逮捕。七十三分钟里，人们躲藏、求救，在恐惧中等待救援。

0–5s: shaky but legible view through empty wet woodland, distant running footsteps implied, no attacker viewpoint. 5–12s: two unidentifiable young adults crouch behind a large rock, one holding a 2011 mobile phone, breathing and moving naturally. No weapons or bodies, no attack choreography.

## 09｜096–108 秒｜湖水另一边的希望
有人躲进树林，有人跳进湖里，试图游向安全的地方。附近居民也驾船赶来救人。面对突如其来的暴力，普通人的援手，为求生者带来了一线希望。

0–5s: wide grey lake with distant adult swimmer silhouette, no drowning closeup. 5–12s: small civilian motorboat with adult rescuers approaches, one throws a flotation ring gently into water. Clear natural hand anatomy, no pulling complex tangled limbs, no shooting.

## 10｜108–120 秒｜不能混淆的数字
这一天，两起袭击共造成七十七人遇难。奥斯陆八人，于特岛六十九人。这不只是一串数字，而是一个个名字，以及再也无法完整团聚的家庭。

0–6s: subdued Oslo memorial, roses laid by adult mourners, no legible labels. 6–12s: quiet island shoreline, single rose in foreground, trees move in breeze. Restrained grief, no bodies or blood, no helicopter spectacle. Leave empty upper space for editorial numeric overlay added later.

## 11｜120–132 秒｜审判不是舞台
二〇一二年，案件进入审判。镜头不该成为凶手传播仇恨的舞台。法庭需要回答的，是他的刑事责任，以及社会应当如何防止危险再次发生。

Modern Norwegian courtroom reconstruction, 2012. 0–6s: wide shot of restrained wood-panelled courtroom, judges seated at plain desks. 6–12s: adult legal professional turns an unlabelled document. No raised arm salute, no speech by attacker, no American jury box, no wigs, no gavel.

## 12｜132–144 秒｜承担刑事责任
二〇一二年八月二十四日，法院作出判决。布雷维克被认定应当承担刑事责任，获判二十一年预防性拘留。注意，这不是二十一年后必然获释。

0–6s: judge in a sober modern Norwegian courtroom consults a folder, adult observers still but breathing. 6–12s: plain prison corridor door under muted window light, camera tracks toward it. No theatrics or identifiable real judges, no readable text, no gavel.

## 13｜144–156 秒｜二十一年不是倒计时
预防性拘留的关键，在于持续评估危险。如果法院认为他仍然威胁社会，拘留期限就可以延长。因此，二十一年并不是一张预先开好的出狱通行证。

0–6s: anonymous adult judicial officials review closed files at a simple desk, restrained procedural realism. 6–12s: prison security gate remains closed while a guard walks away. Metaphorical editorial reconstruction, no imaginary release scene, no smirking face.

## 14｜156–168 秒｜可能终身，不是自动终身
这种延长可以反复进行，因此可能形成终身拘禁。但可能终身，不等于自动关押到死。每一次延长，都需要依法审查，而不是用一句口号代替判决。

One continuous moving shot in a plain contemporary Norwegian secure corridor, anonymous adult guard checks a closed door and walks away; window light shifts gently, natural human motion. No dungeon, torture, chains, exaggerated solitary cell conditions, no claim this depicts an actual prison.

## 15｜168–180 秒｜记住谁
讲述这起案件，不该让凶手成为主角。更值得记住的，是遇难者、幸存者，以及伸出援手的人。面对极端暴力，你认为怎样才能更好地守护无辜者？

0–6s: adult hand places a rose with other flowers at a quiet lakeside remembrance, lake ripples and leaves move. 6–12s: camera rises gently over empty shoreline in subdued dawn light. Dignified hopeful ending, no villain portrait, no celebratory ending, leave central negative space for final editorial question.
